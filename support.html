FAQ

I can't change an app's volume.
  • macOS 14.2 or later is required.
  • Check that VolumeMixer is enabled in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Screen & System Audio Recording. Without it the app shows a warning in the panel and audio plays normally.
  • An app may not be listed until it plays audio; start music or a video.
Why is the slider disabled during a call?

While a voice or video call is active, macOS lowers all other audio. Levels cannot be applied correctly then, so only mute is available. Your saved level returns after the call.

Do you touch apps at 100%?

No. Only apps whose level you change or mute are routed through VolumeMixer. When the level returns to 100% the app is released shortly after.

Is my audio recorded?

No. Audio is processed momentarily only to adjust its level; it is never recorded, stored or sent over the internet. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I reset levels?

Double-click a slider, or reset all levels in Settings › Apps.
