Privacy policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Summary

VolumeMixer has no account, server, analytics or advertising. The app sends no data over the internet and does not track you.

Audio

VolumeMixer uses macOS's system audio recording permission to process the audio of apps whose level you change momentarily and plays it back to your own output device. Audio is never recorded, written to disk or sent off your device. Microphone data is never read.

Data stored on your device

Per-app volume levels, hidden apps, shortcuts and preferences are stored only on your Mac, in the app's own settings storage. Deleting the app removes them.

Third parties

No third-party SDKs, advertising or analytics services are used; no data is shared.

Children

Because the app collects no personal data, it is suitable for users of all ages.
